Course information

Theoretical Foundations & Practical Implementation

Deep Learning is the driving force behind modern AI breakthroughs.

This course covers the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of deep neural networks, including feedforward networks, convolutional neural networks (CNNs), recurrent networks (RNNs/LSTMs), transformer-based architectures, and generative models. Students will train, evaluate, and fine-tune models on real-world datasets using PyTorch and modern deep learning tools.

Reproducibility, error analysis, compute constraints, fairness, privacy, safety, and real-world deployment on public endpoints are treated as core engineering concerns.

16-Week Roadmap

Lecture and Laboratory Schedule

Every week includes lecture slides (viewable directly in browser) and an interactive hands-on lab notebook opening directly in Google Colab.

Week Lecture Topic & Theoretical Concepts Laboratory Activity & Dataset Materials
01 Logistics, Introduction to Deep Learning & Linear Algebra Essentials

Course policies and roadmap; AI vs. ML vs. DL paradigm shift; Data, models, loss functions, and optimization; Scalars, vectors, matrices, dot products, norms, tensor ranks and geometric vector spaces.

 PyTorch Tensor Manipulation, Vector Math & Automatic Differentiation

Google Colab and PyTorch setup; CPU/CUDA GPU allocation; Tensor slicing, reshaping, broadcasting; Autograd computational graphs with first/second-order gradients.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
02 Linear Models to Classification: Linear & Softmax Regression

Linear Regression, MSE loss, Gradient Descent, Learning rate dynamics, and Mini-Batch SGD. Softmax Regression, multiclass classification, One-Hot encoding, Cross-Entropy Loss formulation.

 Linear and Softmax Regression from Scratch & PyTorch nn.Module

Implement Linear Regression from scratch with manual gradient updates; rebuild with PyTorch nn.Linear, nn.MSELoss, and optim.SGD. Build Softmax Regression with nn.CrossEntropyLoss.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
03 Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs), Non-Linearities & Backpropagation Mechanics

Affine transformations, non-linearities, Universal Approximation Theorem; Sigmoid, Tanh, ReLU, LeakyReLU; Computational graphs, reverse-mode autodiff, and the Chain Rule.

 Multilayer Perceptrons (MLPs) & Custom Backpropagation Engine

Implement a 3-layer MLP from scratch with manual analytical backpropagation; rebuild in PyTorch using nn.Sequential and custom nn.Module; benchmark activation functions on Fashion-MNIST.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
04 Numerical Stability, Weight Initialization & Generalization in Deep Learning

Vanishing and exploding gradients; Matrix multiplication dynamics; Symmetry breaking; Xavier/Glorot and He/Kaiming initialization; Training error vs. generalization error, overfitting diagnostics.

 Diagnostic Training: Weight Initialization, Regularization & Overfitting

Evaluate Zero, Normal, Xavier, and He initializations; implement L2 Weight Decay in optimizer and nn.Dropout layers; implement Early Stopping and plot loss curves on Wisconsin Cancer / Bank datasets.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
05 Regularization Techniques for Deep Neural Networks

Weight Decay (L2), L1 Regularization and sparsity; Early Stopping criteria; Dropout mechanics (training vs. inference scaling); Hyperparameter tuning, learning rate schedules, and batch size dynamics.

 Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) & Feature Map Visualizations

Implement 2D Convolution, Max-Pooling, and Batch Normalization in PyTorch (nn.Conv2d, nn.MaxPool2d, nn.BatchNorm2d); build deep CNN; visualize learned filters and feature maps on CIFAR-10.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
06 Convolutional Neural Networks I: Foundations & Spatial Feature Extraction

Computer Vision tasks (Classification, Localization, Detection, Segmentation); 2D cross-correlation, kernels, filters, strides, padding, receptive fields, and pooling layers.

 Deep Residual Networks (ResNet) & Computer Vision Transfer Learning

Implement custom Residual Blocks with skip connections; load pretrained ResNet-18/50 backbones from torchvision; apply data augmentations and fine-tune classifiers on Oxford-IIIT Pet dataset.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
07 Convolutional Neural Networks II: Deep Vision Backbones & Transfer Learning

Evolution of Deep CNNs: LeNet-5, AlexNet, VGG-16/19, GoogLeNet; Residual learning for deep networks (ResNet-18/50/101); Batch Normalization; Feature extraction vs. full fine-tuning.

 Object Detection Metrics & Semantic Segmentation with Mini U-Net

Implement IoU, Precision-Recall curves, and Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS); construct a lightweight U-Net architecture with encoder, bottleneck, and decoder skip connections for segmentation.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
08 Midterm Examination & Sequential Data / Language Modeling Foundations

Midterm assessment of theoretical foundations (Weeks 1–7); Sequential data, Markov chains, N-gram statistical models, Perplexity; Text normalization, BPE and WordPiece tokenization.

 Text Preprocessing, Tokenization & Bag-of-Words / TF-IDF Classification

Build regex cleaning, stopword filtering, and n-gram extraction pipelines; implement BPE and WordPiece with Hugging Face tokenizers; train PyTorch neural classifier on IMDB Sentiment dataset.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
Midterm Checkpoint Theoretical Foundations & Computer Vision Complete · Sequential Models & Modern GenAI Begin
09 Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) & Classical Text Vectorization

Recurrent hidden state update dynamics, Backpropagation Through Time (BPTT), vanishing/exploding gradients; LSTM gates (Forget, Input, Output) and GRUs; Bag-of-Words and TF-IDF representations.

 Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs, LSTMs & GRUs) for Sequence Modeling

Implement Character-level RNN from scratch in PyTorch for sequence generation; build nn.LSTM and nn.GRU models; evaluate perplexity and generate text with temperature sampling.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
10 Dense Word Representations & Word Embeddings

The Distributional Hypothesis; Word2Vec Continuous Bag-of-Words (CBOW) and Continuous Skip-Gram; Negative Sampling and Hierarchical Softmax; Semantic vector arithmetic and cosine similarity.

 Word Embeddings: Word2Vec (Skip-Gram with Negative Sampling) & nn.Embedding

Extract target-context word pairs; train Skip-Gram with Negative Sampling using nn.Embedding lookup tables; compute cosine similarities and visualize 2D word clusters using t-SNE / PCA.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
11 Transformers I: Self-Attention Mechanism & Architecture

Bottlenecks of recurrence; Dot-Product Attention, Query (Q), Key (K), Value (V) projections; Scaled Dot-Product Attention formula; Multi-Head Attention blocks; Sinusoidal Positional Encoding and Transformer Encoder.

 Implementing Scaled Dot-Product & Multi-Head Self-Attention from Scratch

Implement Q, K, V projections and Scaled Dot-Product Attention matrix from scratch; build Multi-Head Attention layer with head splitting/concatenation; construct complete Transformer Encoder block.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
12 Transformers II: Full Architecture & Pretrained Model Families

Autoregressive Decoder, Masked Multi-Head Attention, Encoder-Decoder Cross-Attention; Pretraining paradigms: Masked Language Modeling (BERT, RoBERTa), Causal LM (GPT), Seq2Seq (T5, BART).

 Fine-Tuning Pretrained Transformer Models (BERT & GPT-2)

Load pretrained BERT using Hugging Face transformers; fine-tune for classification with AdamW and linear scheduler; load GPT-2 and run autoregressive generation with Greedy, Top-k, Top-p sampling.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
13 Large Language Models I: Autoregressive Architectures & Generation Dynamics

Decoder-Only LLMs from GPT to modern open models (Llama, Mistral); Context scaling (RoPE, FlashAttention); Autoregressive logits, temperature scaling, beam search, top-p sampling, hallucination mitigation.

 LLM In-Context Prompting & Building a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) System

Implement zero-shot, few-shot, and Chain-of-Thought (CoT) prompting; build local RAG with document parsing, recursive chunking, sentence-transformers, ChromaDB / FAISS, and grounded generation.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
14 Large Language Models II: Scaling Laws, In-Context Learning & Prompting

Empirical compute/dataset scaling laws (Chinchilla); Emergent abilities; In-context learning; Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture, semantic search, and context injection.

 Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) with LoRA and QLoRA

Load quantized LLM in 4-bit precision via bitsandbytes; configure and inject Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) adapters with Hugging Face peft; fine-tune with trl.SFTTrainer on instruction datasets.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
15 Fine-Tuning Large Models & Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT)

Full fine-tuning bottlenecks & catastrophic forgetting; Adapters, Prefix/Prompt Tuning, LoRA and QLoRA mathematics; Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) and preference alignment (RLHF, DPO).

 Diffusion Models: Forward Noise Schedules & Text-to-Image Generation

Implement DDPM forward noise addition across timesteps; train toy U-Net diffusion model to predict noise; execute text-conditioned image synthesis with Hugging Face diffusers (Stable Diffusion).
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
16 Diffusion Models for Generative AI & Course Synthesis

Generative AI landscape (VAEs vs. GANs vs. Diffusion); Forward noising & reverse denoising dynamics; Text-conditioned Latent Diffusion (Stable Diffusion); Capstone project evaluations and course synthesis.

 Deep Learning Model Optimization, ONNX Export & Interactive UI Deployment

Export PyTorch models to ONNX / TorchScript; benchmark latency, throughput, memory, and CPU vs. GPU; build interactive Gradio / Streamlit demo and deploy publicly to Hugging Face Spaces.
📄 Slides (PDF) ⚡ Open Colab
