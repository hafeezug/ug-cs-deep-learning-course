Course information

Build the models. Understand the choices.

Deep Learning develops the mathematical and computational foundations of modern neural networks and their responsible application.

Students implement, train, regularize, evaluate, explain, and deploy PyTorch models for vision, text, time-series, generative, and graph problems. Reproducibility, error analysis, compute constraints, fairness, privacy, safety, and environmental impact are treated as core engineering concerns.

Learning outcomes

What students will be able to do

CLO 1 · C2

Explain

Forward and backward computation, optimization, and the principles behind major deep-learning model families.

CLO 2 · C3

Implement

Train PyTorch models for vision, text, time-series, and graph data using reproducible workflows.

CLO 3 · C4

Analyze

Use metrics, learning curves, ablations, error analysis, and computational cost to diagnose models.

CLO 4 · C6

Design

Create an end-to-end pipeline for transfer learning, tuning, deployment, and monitoring.

CLO 5 · C5

Evaluate

Assess interpretability, fairness, privacy, safety, environmental, and societal implications.
